# Sparktex

Hi hi hi! I'm Sarah Park (spark), and I'm a rising senior in high school. I'm super into learning and trying random new things, whether that be in STEM or the humanities. As of right now, this site is mostly a record of my thoughts/things I find interesting.

I used to be a weird bio/math kid, though after my first science fair in 10th grade, I got super into biomedical engineering. (For a reason why, I'd be hyped to tell you if you dm me.) I then did a bunch of BME projects I thought were cool, and did some more science fairs.

Around this site, you'll find some things that I care about, including, but not limited to, building random stuff, learning cool proofs, and reading funny Latin. 

This site's main purpose is to catalog the various somewhat non-serious projects I work on. Most notably, I have been super into analog computing, which is currently the most serious of my non-serious projects. However, I also have a page for some day-to-day occurrences/thoughts, as well as a page dedicated to interesting things that I will hopefully write up in LaTeX. (Credit to my RSI friends for showing me the beauty of LaTeX formatting.)

Unfortunately, I'm also a huge fan of several other things that don't lend themselves as well to being written up, which includes figure skating, K-pop, and origami. Maybe one day I'll figure out how to add this too.
